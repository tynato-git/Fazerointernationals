# Fazero International Website

## Goal
Build a premium, multi-page business-growth consultancy website that presents Fazero’s services, verified client results, international positioning, and consultation path without making unsupported claims.

## What will be built
- A polished homepage with sticky navigation, animated growth-system hero, credibility metrics, services, growth framework, search/AI discoverability, global presence, verified results, resources, consultation call-to-action, and full footer.
- A dedicated Case Studies page with all five documented programs, industry filtering, expandable details, funnels, timelines, and the required results disclaimer.
- Services, About, Resources, Presence, and Contact pages with clear internal navigation and direct-answer content.
- Eight useful location pages for Trichy, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, Dubai, UAE, Malaysia, and USA, each with original market-specific copy rather than duplicated doorway content.
- A dedicated India-to-Dubai growth resource plus the requested resource topics.
- Privacy Policy, Terms, and Cookie Policy pages with clearly marked editable legal placeholders.
- A branded not-found experience consistent with the site.

## Visual direction
- Use the specified warm beige, soft gold, deep charcoal, and off-white palette as semantic design tokens.
- Pair editorial typography with restrained technical labels and data-forward layouts.
- Use thin-line diagrams, moving nodes, route paths, funnels, and subtle data animations as the signature visual language.
- Keep motion accessible, lightweight, and disabled or simplified when reduced motion is preferred.
- Avoid generic agency styling, exaggerated effects, stock imagery, fabricated office claims, and unverified social links.

## Content and conversion
- Preserve every verified portfolio figure exactly as supplied.
- Clearly separate client-confirmed metrics from explanatory copy and include the required individual-results disclaimer.
- Present services through practical growth outcomes: acquisition, visibility, systems, AI adoption, sales, and market expansion.
- Build a complete consultation form with all specified fields; submission will validate in-page and provide a mail-based handoff because no persistent backend was requested.
- Add the supplied email and phone contact options throughout key conversion points.

## Search and structure
- Add unique route-level titles, descriptions, Open Graph fields, Twitter cards, canonical paths, semantic headings, breadcrumbs, and relevant JSON-LD.
- Structure direct answers and FAQs for SEO, AEO, and GEO while avoiding keyword stuffing and fabricated ratings, addresses, reviews, or credentials.
- Preserve crawler access. A sitemap will remain deferred until a public site URL exists, avoiding a placeholder domain.

## Technical details
- Use the existing TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn, and Lucide stack rather than the brief’s Next.js request, since this project’s framework is fixed.
- Create shared site-shell, motion-line, metrics, service, case-study, form, and SEO data modules to keep all pages consistent.
- Use CSS/SVG animation and React state for filters, accordions, and menus; no backend or external API is required.
- Verify the key consultation and case-study interactions in the browser at desktop and mobile widths, then confirm the preview build is clean.
